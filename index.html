A quick rule of thumb for the dates

  • 16–20 Dec (you two): the Barranc de l’Infern, Peñón de Ifach or Sierra Espuña, plus Tabarca if the boats run.
  • 21–23 Dec (with Mom): the pink lake, Elche, Calblanque & Cabo de Palos, Alicante. Shop for Christmas on the 23rd.
  • 24–25 Dec: plan around nature. Most sights, shops and many restaurants close on the afternoon of the 24th and all of the 25th, but beaches and trails don’t.
  • 24–29 Dec: one multi-day trip fits here, ending back in Torrevieja (ALC is 35 min from the house). Mom’s flight on the 29th may mean coming home on the 28th.

What’s on around your dates

Checked on 18 Nov 2026. Most 2026 programmes come out in early December, so check again before you go.

Dec – 6 Jan

Christmas in Alicante

Alicante centre · 45 min

A Christmas craft market on Paseo Federico Soto, plus lights along the Rambla and the Explanada. In recent years the city also built a monumental nativity scene with giant figures in front of the Town Hall. Go up to Santa Bárbara castle for sunset over the bay, then walk down through the old Santa Cruz quarter.

Market listed for 1 Dec – 6 Jan (another listing says 20 Dec – 5 Jan). The 2026 nativity scene isn’t confirmed yet.

26 – 30 Dec

Venida de la Virgen

Elche & Santa Pola · 35–40 min

Elche re-enacts the legend of the Virgin’s statue washing up on the beach in 1370. 28 Dec, before dawn: a pilgrimage walks from Tamarit beach in Santa Pola to Elche. 29 Dec: the main procession, with dozens of children dressed as angels. It has regional tourist-interest status, and pairs well with the Palmeral.

Listed for 26–30 Dec 2026. The hour-by-hour programme isn’t out yet.

28 Dec

Els Enfarinats

Ibi · 1 h 10

On the Day of the Holy Innocents (Spain’s April Fools’ Day), the “Enfarinats” stage a mock coup, run the town for a day and “fine” people, all in a battle of flour, eggs and firecrackers. It’s about 200 years old and very photogenic. Watch from the edges or wear clothes you can throw away. Xixona, the turrón town, is 15 minutes away for an afternoon visit.

Always held on 28 Dec. The 2026 programme isn’t out yet.

31 Dec

New Year’s Eve

Any town square

Eat 12 grapes on the 12 chimes at midnight. The biggest party nearby is in Alicante (Town Hall square and Postiguet beach). Torrevieja has its own celebration on the seafront. Restaurants mostly offer set menus that night, so book early.

The car plan returns the car on 30 Dec, so either extend the rental or take a taxi that night.

3 – 5 Jan 2027

Three Kings in Alcoy

Alcoy · 1 h 20

Possibly the oldest Three Kings parade in Spain, protected as intangible cultural heritage. It runs over several days: Les Pastoretes (3 Jan), the Bando Real (4 Jan), then the big evening Cabalgata on 5 Jan, when ladders go up to the balconies to deliver gifts, ending with fireworks. Only relevant if you stay into January.

Parade listed for 5 Jan 2027. Times at alcoyturismo.com.

Holidays that change opening hours

  • 24 Dec: shops and sights close mid-afternoon, and many restaurants close for the evening, which is family dinner (Nochebuena).
  • 25 Dec & 1 Jan: almost everything is closed, including supermarkets. Stock up the day before.
  • 31 Dec: early closing. 5 Jan evening: Kings parades everywhere, Torrevieja included. 6 Jan: public holiday.

Practical notes

  • Car: the recommended rental (Hertz at ALC, 16–30 Dec; not booked yet) covers all of this. Three adults fit fine in an Economy car. Extend the rental if you stay past the 30th.
  • Daylight: sunrise ≈ 08:10, sunset ≈ 17:50 on the coast. Start big hikes by 09:00.
  • Book soon: Christmas-week Airbnbs in San José, Albarracín and Cuenca old town are limited. Most allow free cancellation until a few days before.
  • Check closer to the date: the Tabarca boats, Fonts de l’Algar winter hours, Oasys/Fort Bravo winter days, Ciudad Encantada holiday hours, and the 2026 programmes for Ibi and Elche.
  • Weather: the coast is usually 16–18 °C and sunny, but a cold, wet spell (a DANA) can bring a day or two of heavy rain. Keep Murcia, Cartagena or Valencia as rainy-day backups.
