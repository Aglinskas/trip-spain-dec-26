Spain in December

Ideas for the Torrevieja trip: festivals happening around your dates, day trips from Mom’s house, and four ways to spend 24–29 Dec away with Airbnbs. Everything in western Andalusia is left out, since you’ve already done Málaga, Granada, Gibraltar, Cádiz and Seville. Every place name opens in Google Maps.

A quick rule of thumb for the dates

  • 16–20 Dec (you two): the Barranc de l’Infern, Peñón de Ifach or Sierra Espuña, plus Tabarca if the boats run.
  • 21–23 Dec (with Mom): half-day outings: the pink lake, the Elche Palmeral, Calblanque & Cabo de Palos, Alicante castle. Shop for Christmas on the 23rd.
  • 24–25 Dec: plan around nature. Most sights, shops and many restaurants close on the afternoon of the 24th and all of the 25th, but beaches and trails don’t.
  • 26–29 Dec: the Elche festival (26–30) and Ibi’s flour battle (28) fall in the middle of the trip. Option B can reach Ibi easily.

What’s on around your dates

Checked on 18 Nov 2026. Most 2026 programmes come out in early December, so check again before you go.

Dec – 6 Jan

Christmas in Alicante

Alicante centre · 45 min

A Christmas craft market on Paseo Federico Soto, plus lights along the Rambla and the Explanada. In recent years the city also built a monumental nativity scene with giant figures in front of the Town Hall. Go up to Santa Bárbara castle for sunset over the bay, then walk down through the old Santa Cruz quarter.

Market listed for 1 Dec – 6 Jan (another listing says 20 Dec – 5 Jan). The 2026 nativity scene isn’t confirmed yet.

26 – 30 Dec

Venida de la Virgen

Elche & Santa Pola · 35–40 min

Elche re-enacts the legend of the Virgin’s statue washing up on the beach in 1370. 28 Dec, before dawn: a pilgrimage walks from Tamarit beach in Santa Pola to Elche. 29 Dec: the main procession, with dozens of children dressed as angels. It has regional tourist-interest status, and pairs well with the Palmeral.

Listed for 26–30 Dec 2026. The hour-by-hour programme isn’t out yet.

28 Dec

Els Enfarinats

Ibi · 1 h 10

On the Day of the Holy Innocents (Spain’s April Fools’ Day), the “Enfarinats” stage a mock coup, run the town for a day and “fine” people, all in a battle of flour, eggs and firecrackers. It’s about 200 years old and very photogenic. Watch from the edges or wear clothes you can throw away. Xixona, the turrón town, is 15 minutes away for an afternoon visit.

Always held on 28 Dec. The 2026 programme isn’t out yet.

31 Dec

New Year’s Eve

Any town square

Eat 12 grapes on the 12 chimes at midnight. The biggest party nearby is in Alicante (Town Hall square and Postiguet beach). Torrevieja has its own celebration on the seafront. Restaurants mostly offer set menus that night, so book early.

The car plan returns the car on 30 Dec, so either extend the rental or take a taxi that night.

3 – 5 Jan 2027

Three Kings in Alcoy

Alcoy · 1 h 20

Possibly the oldest Three Kings parade in Spain, protected as intangible cultural heritage. It runs over several days: Les Pastoretes (3 Jan), the Bando Real (4 Jan), then the big evening Cabalgata on 5 Jan, when ladders go up to the balconies to deliver gifts, ending with fireworks. Only relevant if you stay into January.

Parade listed for 5 Jan 2027. Times at alcoyturismo.com.

Holidays that change opening hours

  • 24 Dec: shops and sights close mid-afternoon, and many restaurants close for the evening, which is family dinner (Nochebuena).
  • 25 Dec & 1 Jan: almost everything is closed, including supermarkets. Stock up the day before.
  • 31 Dec: early closing. 5 Jan evening: Kings parades everywhere, Torrevieja included. 6 Jan: public holiday.

Day trips under an hour

Easy half or full days, good for 21–23 Dec with Mom. Drive times are approximate, without traffic.

Pink salt lake & La Mata

🚗 10 mineasy walks

Torrevieja’s salt lake turns pink from algae, best on a sunny afternoon. The Las Lagunas de La Mata park next door has flat trails and bird hides, and flamingos spend the winter here. Free, and good for the first evening.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Palmeral of Elche

🚗 35 mineasy walks

A UNESCO World Heritage palm grove of about 200,000 palms. Visit the Huerto del Cura garden with its eight-armed Imperial Palm, walk the signed Ruta del Palmeral, then see the Basilica of Santa María. Go 26–29 Dec and you also catch the Venida festival.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Tabarca island

🚗 30 min + boateasy walks

Spain’s smallest inhabited island: a walled 18th-century village, a marine reserve and a lighthouse walk, with fish caldero for lunch. The boat from Santa Pola takes about 25 minutes. Winter boats run less often and are cancelled in rough seas, and some operators stop in Jan–Feb. Phone ahead.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Calblanque & Cabo de Palos

🚗 55 minbeach walks

The best wild beaches near you: dunes, fossilised sandstone and no buildings at all. Walk Playa Larga → Las Cañas → Parreño, then go up to the Cabo de Palos lighthouse and have a fish lunch by the harbour. Probably the best beach day of the trip.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Cartagena

🚗 50 mincity

A 2,000-year-old port. The Roman theatre museum is excellent, and the streets are full of modernist buildings. Add the Batería de Castillitos, giant coastal guns in fortress casemates on a cliff (30 min further), for a dramatic viewpoint stop.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Novelda: the Magdalena sanctuary

🚗 45 minshort stop

A modernist church in the style of Gaudí, built of local stone, next to the Moorish Castillo de la Mola with its unusual triangular tower. A good short stop; the Casa-Museo Modernista in town is worth adding.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Alicante: castle & old town

🚗 45 mincity

Take the lift up into Santa Bárbara castle for the view over the bay, wander the blue-and-white Santa Cruz quarter, the Explanada palm promenade and the Christmas market. Works with an airport run.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Murcia

🚗 45 mincity

A baroque cathedral, the extravagant Casino of Murcia, and tapas on Plaza de las Flores, plus Christmas lights. A good choice for a rainy or windy day.

Bigger days, 1 – 2 hours

Full days. The harder hikes suit 16–20 Dec, before Mom arrives.

Guadalest, the Algar waterfalls & Altea

🚗 1 h 20 – 1 h 35easy walks

The classic Costa Blanca loop. Guadalest is a castle village on a crag above a turquoise reservoir. Fonts de l’Algar has waterfalls and pools (paid entry; check winter hours). Altea is a white old town with a blue-domed church; stay for sunset.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Peñón de Ifach, Calpe

🚗 1 h 25medium hike

A 332 m limestone rock rising straight out of the sea. The path goes through a tunnel to the summit, and the last part is a scramble that closes when wet. At the bottom is a salt flat with flamingos. Check whether access needs booking that day.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Barranc de l’Infern

🚗 1 h 45big hike

Known as “the cathedral of hiking”: a 14 km loop from Fleix through a canyon on about 6,000 old Moorish stone steps, taking 5–7 hours. This is your Caminito del Rey-type day. Best done 16–20 Dec, just the two of you.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Bolnuevo & the Mazarrón coast

🚗 1 h 05short walks

The Gredas de Bolnuevo: wind-carved sandstone shaped like mushrooms, right on the beach. South of here, a dirt road runs past empty coves towards Cabo Cope. It’s also the first stop on Option A.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Villena castle & Bocairent

🚗 1 h 05 / 1 h 25villages

Villena: the Atalaya castle on its hill, and the Treasure of Villena (Bronze Age gold) in the museum. Bocairent: a village stacked on a cliff, and the Covetes dels Moros, dozens of man-made cave windows cut into a rock face.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Cañón de Almadenes

🚗 1 h 20gorge

A gorge carved by the Segura river, with viewpoints along the rim, guided visits to the Cueva de la Serreta rock-art cave, and rafting or kayaking trips on some days. It’s on the edge of the Calasparra rice country.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Sierra Espuña

🚗 1 h 20forest hikes

A pine-covered mountain park with the old snow wells (Pozos de la Nieve), viewpoints and forest trails, plus the village of Aledo. In December it can be frosty or snowy at the top.

Open in Google Maps ↗

A

Almería: Cabo de Gata, the desert & a cave house

The warmest and sunniest option, and the one closest to your taste: volcanic beaches with no buildings, Europe’s only desert, and a night in a cave house. It’s all new to you, and nature carries Christmas Day when everything else is shut.

    Coast road south

    Torrevieja → Gredas de Bolnuevo → lunch in Águilas under its castle → Mojácar white village at sunset → San José. Restaurants close early tonight, so shop on the 23rd and cook in.

    🚗 ≈ 3 h 15 driving in total
  2. Fri 25Christmas Day
    Cabo de Gata beaches

    Everything is closed, but the beaches aren’t. Walk the coast path from San José to Los Genoveses and Mónsul (≈ 8 km round trip, flat), then go on to Cala de la Media Luna and Cala Carbón.

    🚗 No driving needed
  3. Sat 26
    Lighthouse & the east

    The Cabo de Gata lighthouse and the Arrecife de las Sirenas rocks, then the flamingo salt pans. In the afternoon: the Mirador de la Amatista, Los Escullos, and Las Negras or Agua Amarga.

    🚗 ≈ 1 h 30 driving
  4. Sun 27
    Almería & the desert

    The Alcazaba of Almería (check holiday hours) → Tabernas desert: canyons and old spaghetti-western film sets (Oasys / Fort Bravo) → drive on to a cave house in Guadix.

    🚗 ≈ 2 h 30 driving
  5. Mon 28
    Gorafe badlands

    The Gorafe desert: canyons of eroded badlands and 240 Neolithic dolmens. Back in Guadix, the cathedral and the troglodyte quarter, with around 2,000 inhabited caves.

    🚗 ≈ 1 h driving
  6. Tue 29Mom flies
    Back to Alicante

    Guadix → ALC is ≈ 3 h 15 via Lorca. Check Mom’s flight time: for a morning flight, end in San José instead and leave on the 28th.

My pick

Option A (Almería) fits what you enjoy most: road trips with viewpoints, wild beaches, and warm, sunny weather. If Mom would rather not move around, B (Jávea) is the relaxed version, and C is the one to pick if you want a dramatic winter feel.

Practical notes

  • Car: the recommended rental (Hertz at ALC, 16–30 Dec; not booked yet) covers all of this. Three adults fit fine in an Economy car. Extend the rental if you stay past the 30th.
  • Daylight: sunrise ≈ 08:10, sunset ≈ 17:50 on the coast. Start big hikes by 09:00.
  • Book soon: Christmas-week Airbnbs in San José, Albarracín and Cuenca old town are limited. Most allow free cancellation until a few days before.
  • Check closer to the date: the Tabarca boats, Fonts de l’Algar winter hours, Oasys/Fort Bravo winter days, Ciudad Encantada holiday hours, and the 2026 programmes for Ibi and Elche.
  • Weather: the coast is usually 16–18 °C and sunny, but a cold, wet spell (a DANA) can bring a day or two of heavy rain. Keep Murcia, Cartagena or Valencia as rainy-day backups.
